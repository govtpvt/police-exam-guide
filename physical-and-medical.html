Police Physical Tests and Medical Examination: What to Expect

More candidates leave police recruitment at the ground than in the exam hall. The physical stages are simple to understand and hard to fake, so preparation has to start with the application, not after the written result.

Physical standard test (PST)

A measurement of height, and for men chest unexpanded and expanded, with weight checked against height. Standards are set separately for men and women and are relaxed for candidates from hill areas, certain tribes and specified regions. If you are measured below the standard you can usually appeal on the spot for a re-measurement by a different officer.

Physical efficiency test (PET)

EventWhat to know
RunA fixed distance within a time limit that differs by gender and age group. Many boards time it electronically with a chip.
Long jump and high jumpA minimum distance or height, with a limited number of attempts.
Other eventsSome states add shot put, rope climbing or chin-ups.

The PET is qualifying in most recruitments, but some states award marks for faster times, so check whether it affects merit. Pregnant candidates are generally given a deferred test under the rules in the notification.

Medical examination

  • Vision: a minimum distant vision in each eye, normally without glasses, and normal colour vision.
  • Limbs and posture: knock knees, flat feet, bow legs and varicose veins are common grounds for rejection.
  • General health: hearing, blood pressure, and absence of chronic disease.
  • Tattoos: several forces restrict their size, content and location on the body.

A candidate declared unfit can apply for a review medical board within the period stated, with a certificate from a specialist.

Preparing sensibly

  1. Begin running three to four months before the expected test date and build distance gradually.
  2. Practise on a track of the same surface if you can.
  3. Get an eye test and a basic health check early, while there is time to act on the result.
  4. Carry the documents named in the call letter; candidates are turned away for missing originals.

Schedules for physical tests are released as separate notices, often with a new admit card. Results of each stage appear on the results page. More guides: govtpvt.com.
