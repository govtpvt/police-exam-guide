# Police Exam Guide

A short guide to police recruitment in India: who recruits, the stages of selection, and the physical and medical tests. Published by [Govtpvt](https://govtpvt.com/).

Live site: https://police-exam-guide.val.run/

## Pages

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | State police boards, SSC for Delhi Police and CAPFs, qualifications and stages |
| `physical-and-medical.html` | Physical standard test, physical efficiency test, medical examination |

## Who this is for

First-time applicants for constable and sub-inspector posts who want to know what happens after the written exam, where most candidates drop out.

## Before you apply

1. Measure yourself against the physical standards for your category and region.
2. Check domicile and language rules for state police.
3. Note the age cut-off date.

New notifications are listed under [government job notifications](https://govtpvt.com/govt-job/latest-jobs) and hall tickets under [admit cards](https://govtpvt.com/govt-job/admit-cards).

Standards differ between forces and change over time; the official notification is the authority.
