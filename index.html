Police Recruitment in India: Constable and Sub-Inspector Exams

Police jobs are among the most applied-for government posts in India, and also among the most misunderstood, because recruitment is split between the states and the centre. This guide sets out who recruits, what the stages are and where candidates most often drop out. Open notifications are listed on the Govtpvt latest government jobs page.

Typical qualifications

  • Constable: class 10 or 12, depending on the force.
  • Sub-inspector: a graduate degree.
  • Driver, wireless, technical and ministerial cadres: a licence, ITI trade or specific subjects as notified.

Stages of selection

  1. Written exam, now usually computer based
  2. Physical standard test: height, chest and weight measurements
  3. Physical efficiency test: timed run and, in many states, jumps or other events
  4. Document verification
  5. Medical examination
  6. Character and antecedent verification before appointment

The order varies: some boards hold the physical tests first to reduce numbers. The physical and medical stages are covered on the physical and medical page.

Before you apply

  • Measure yourself against the physical standards for your category and region.
  • For state police, check domicile and local language requirements.
  • Note the age cut-off date; police posts have lower upper age limits than most civil posts.

Then follow the admit card, answer key and result lists through each stage.
